Build with AI. Then connect it to the real world.

GDG Phoenix and GDG on Campus ASU are bringing developers, students, engineers, researchers, and curious builders together, alongside the building and experimenting happening at sunhacks.

Expect practical ways to build with AI, develop software, and connect intelligent applications to the physical world through IoT and edge computing.

  • Students working on a first project

Getting there

Park on Apache Blvd, check in at the Student Pavilion, then walk over to LSE 104 for the talks. Everything is within a 10-minute walk.

  1. 1
    Park

    Apache Blvd Parking Structure

    401 E Lemon St, Tempe. Parking is not free, even on Saturday. Pull a ticket when you drive in and hang on to it: it has a QR code. Bring it to check-in at the Student Pavilion and we can scan it to cover your parking. Reimbursements are limited, so if they run out, pay by card before you leave. ASU charges $4 an hour, up to $16 (current rates).
