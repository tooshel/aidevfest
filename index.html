Build with AI. Then connect it to the real world.

GDG Phoenix and GDG on Campus ASU are bringing developers, students, engineers, researchers, and curious builders together, alongside the building and experimenting happening at SunHacks.

Expect practical ways to build with AI, develop software, and connect intelligent applications to the physical world through IoT and edge computing.

  • Experienced developers looking to go deeper
  • Students working on a first project
  • SunHacks hackers looking for ideas and tools
  • Anyone simply curious about AI and emerging technology
